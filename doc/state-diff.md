# State-construction diff: the engine state rebuilt from a player's view vs Showdown's real state

The search's root state and the training data's inputs are both engine states we rebuild from the protocol a player receives (log + request): poke-env parses the log, `agents/timers.py` counts what the log shows, and `agents/engine_state.build_state` assembles poke-engine's `State`. The engine diff (`doc/engine-diff.md`) only verifies that "starting from the real state, the engine plays out the same as Showdown"; it **does not verify this step**: it builds the engine state straight from Showdown's complete internal state. This document tracks the consistency of the rebuilding step.

## Conclusions (2026-10-24)

- 600 real OU games (random pairings from the team catalog), 45,219 decision points:
  - The code at the time of the v7.3 data build (`ae86d1e`): **42.6%** of the decision points have at least one mismatch, **10.5%** in core fields (HP, status, boosts, moves and PP, item, ability, active Pokémon, field); the biggest single item is the HP of the opponent's benched Pokémon (Regenerator's healing, the bookkeeping while Zoroark is disguised).
  - Now: **0.17%** (core fields 0.02%).
- Million scale (about 1.08 million decision points per round, OU + 40 fixtures sets + 30 big sets): first round 0.23% → seventh round (new seeds) **0.0010%** (11 decision points), core fields **0.0002%**, i.e. 99.999% agreement.
- The replay path (spectator, the one the training data uses): second round (new seeds) **0.011%**; `enginecheck/replaybuild.py`, `scripts/check_state_build.py --path replay` -- built from the spectator log the way `train/positions.py` does it (the SpectatorSide of a whole-game parse), both sides' hidden sets replaced by the real sets. First million-scale round 0.66% / 0.65% / 0.43% (OU / fixtures / big); after fixing S143–S145 and the comparison framework, all example games agree (see below). Not covered: real ladder replays give HP as percentages (exact values in the diff). The original sentence: the replay path shares poke-env, the timers and most of `build_state`; its own parts (sets known for the whole game, Illusion in replays) had not yet been checked separately at the time, see "Follow-ups".
- Causality (plan B, 10-24): the training samples' history / reveal flags / ratings / turn = computed on the live path from the log up to that decision point; 3,000 real replays, 0 differences; the history extended inside the search tree agrees with the log, 2,000 games, 64,098 steps, only 0.24% left in structural fields (Illusion, Minior's color, Revival Blessing, see below); result fields (damage, who moved first, etc.) are not filled in the tree, and adding them must go through a search A/B. Along the way the parser's C5–C10 were fixed (shared by training and live play).
- Unified conversion layer (plan C, 10-24): reads of poke-env are gathered in `agents/pokeview` (own side / current side separate, enforced by an AST test), actions have a single representation (`agents/slots`, slots computed only by Rust); a round-trip check runs at every decision point of A. Fixed along the way: sampling the impossible set Assault Vest + status move (P1), and Greninja-Bond failing to convert to a command (P2).
- Values set at the start (plan D, 10-24): checked item by item against Showdown's source; everything that needs rebuilding is recorded when the event happens; what is left are engine gaps (binding items, consecutive-use multipliers, Laser Focus, Stockpile at +6, Uproar's lock).
- Tests: `tests/test_state_build.py` pins 60 random-set games and 60 OU games (`data/teams/statebuild_catalog_pairs.jsonl`, generated locally, not committed) to baselines (`tests/fixtures/state_build_baseline*.json`: no new categories allowed, no category's count may grow); every fix also has a targeted test (`tests/test_timers.py`, `tests/test_engine_state.py`).

## Method

- **How we compare**: `scripts/check_state_build.py` (`enginecheck/statebuild.py`; the runner is still `enginecheck/random_battle.js`, with `views` added: each player's protocol lines (Showdown's `|split|` resolved) and the request at each decision point). At each decision point, feed poke-env in the server's actual order (log first, then request: `server/room-battle.ts` sends `update` before `sideupdate`), call `build_state`, and compare with `enginecheck.engine_state(real state)`.
- **Oracle mode (default)**: the opponent's hidden set (item, ability, moves, stats, tera type) is replaced by the real set (`statebuild._oracle`), so every difference from belief sampling disappears and whatever remains must be a construction error; this also lets the opponent's hidden fields be compared. `--belief` turns it off.
- **What we compare**: our whole side (each Pokémon's HP, stats, status, item, current / base ability, moves with PP and disabled flags, tera, sleep counter, Transform backup, last item; the side's volatiles and counters, side conditions and their remaining turns, boosts, Substitute, Wish / Future Sight, last move), the opponent's public part (the same, plus the hidden part in oracle mode), the field (weather / terrain / the rooms and their remaining turns).
- **Not counted as errors (the player cannot know it, or the two sides only write it differently)**:
  - durations Showdown draws at random (confusion, partial trapping, the length of locking moves: `HIDDEN_LENGTH`), only their presence is compared;
  - stats copied by Transform (the request only gives one's own); how much HP a Substitute that took hits has left;
  - the opponent's disguised Zoroark (whether this world drew it is belief sampling); a disguise switched out without being revealed (in real battles HP is a percentage, you cannot tell);
  - the other fields of fainted Pokémon (the forme is rebuilt; nothing reads them); the disabled flags of an active Pokémon about to be switched out and of the "must recharge" turn (Showdown's flags are from the last move request);
  - Future Sight's user compared by species (the two sides order the team differently); the tera flag "this side has used tera" compared per side; Greninja-Bond treated as Greninja; items the engine does not know all treated as UNKNOWNITEM;
  - an Outrage lock that ends in confusion (when already confused, the end of the lock is not logged); the end of the lock of an Own Tempo Pokémon (it cannot be confused, likewise not logged);
  - flinch: a random roll, added silently, shown only when it stops a move.
  - a Zoroark that faints while disguised: the log's faint names the disguise, unknowable at the time.
- **Fixes to the diff framework itself (continued)**: when oracle mode injects the opponent's real set under Power Trick, Showdown's stats are already swapped; inject the unswapped ones (the same as taking the backup under Transform).
- **Fixes to the diff framework itself**: for switch decisions after the end of the turn (after `|upkeep|`, before the next `|turn|`), the turn number `engine_state` uses to work out Future Sight / Wish must be one higher (the engine diff only compares turn starts and never ran into it).
- **Data**: `data/teams/statebuild_catalog_pairs.jsonl` (1,176 real OU teams in random pairings, generated locally), `tests/fixtures/random_battle_teams*.jsonl` (40 sets, 11,600 pairs), `data/teams/big/` (30 sets, 87,000 pairs). The same pair of teams with a different seed is a new game.
- **Workflow**: run a batch → sort by "how many games it appears in" → look at examples (log + both sides' values) → confirm the rule against Showdown's source → fix → write a targeted test (it must fail before the fix) → regression (full test suite + both baselines + engine diff) → record it in the table below.
- **Scripts**: the million-scale sweep `scripts/state_build_sweep.sh <live|replay> <OU seed offset> <fixtures / big seed offset> <label>` (new seeds each round); rerunning a report's example games `scripts/rerun_state_build.py <report…> [--list category]` (picks the team pairing by `pairs[seed − offset]`, the same as the sweep).

## Unified conversion layer (plan C)

- **The layer that reads poke-env**: `agents/pokeview.py`. poke-env mixes a Pokémon's own values with its current ones (after Transform, `moves`, `base_stats` and `weight` are the target's; with a temporary ability, `ability` is the temporary one); this was a recurring source of errors in the recheck rounds. Every function in this layer states which one it reads: `own_*` (its own, from the set) and `current_*` (what it is in battle now) -- `own_moves` / `current_moves`, `own_base_stats`, `own_weight`, `own_stats`, `own_ability` / `current_ability`, `engine_ability` (what the engine uses: the temporary one first), `copied_ability`, `copied_pp`, `last_used_move`. The helpers of the same names in `agents/engine_state.py` moved here, and direct reads were all replaced by calls to them (behaviour unchanged).
- **Check**: `tests/test_pokeview.py` scans `engine_state.py` with the AST: `moves`, `ability`, `base_ability`, `base_stats`, `stats`, `weight`, `temporary_ability` etc. may only be read on engine / set objects (the listed receivers, each confirmed not to be a poke-env Pokémon; engine objects with clashing names were renamed: `engine_mon`, `em`, `setter`). Adding one direct read makes it fail.
- **The single representation of actions**: `agents/slots.py`. An action's name (the engine's option name: "earthquake", "earthquake-tera", "switch greattusk", "struggle") is the one intermediate representation between the network's slots, the search's labels and battle commands; slots are computed only in Rust (`features::slot` / `slot_in`), and Python looks names up in `slot_names`. The training data (`train/positions`) used to compute them again itself (move index + 4 for tera, 8 + team index for switches, whether or not it was a legal option); deleted; `engine_move_to_order` became an alias of `slots.order_of`.
- **Round-trip check**: at every decision point of A (`enginecheck/statebuild.option_diffs`): the set of option names of the constructed state = that of the real state (Greninja-Bond compared as Greninja, consistent with the comparator); every option name converts to a command for this battle. The baseline test pins its categories.

## Values set when an effect starts (plan D)

In Showdown, one class of values is fixed the moment an effect starts and no longer follows the state afterwards: `durationCallback` (the duration from the item / ability at the start), and the values `onStart` writes into `effectState` (a Substitute's HP, Wish's healing amount, the locked move…). Rebuilding them from the "current" state gets them wrong (e.g. after Reflect with Light Clay, the item is knocked off, and the remaining turns counted as 5 -- fixed in the 10-23 recheck). The list was scanned from Showdown's source (every `durationCallback` and assignment to `this.effectState.*` in `data/moves.ts`, `conditions.ts`, `abilities.ts`, `items.ts`, 139 items), each checked against how we record it; "A compares" means the state-construction diff (plan A) compares this field, at 99.999% at million scale.

| value | when Showdown fixes it | us | A compares | conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| turns of weather / terrain / screens (Damp / Heat / Smooth / Icy Rock, Terrain Extender, Light Clay: 8) | the setter's item at the start (`durationCallback`) | `timers.set_items`: record the setter's item the moment it is set; later item changes and suppression (Magic Room, Embargo) all go by that time | remaining turns | agree |
| turns of Trick Room, Gravity, Magic / Wonder Room, Tailwind, Safeguard, Aurora Veil | `durationCallback` only adds turns for Gen 4's Persistent | fixed turns | remaining turns | not relevant in Gen 9 |
| Taunt's turns | +1 if the target has already acted this turn at the start (`onStart`) | `taunt = -1` (when already acted) | remaining turns | agree |
| Heal Block's turns | Psychic Noise 2, others 5 | the engine only has 2 (only Psychic Noise) | presence | engine limitation; Gen 9 has only Psychic Noise |
| Wish's healing amount | half the user's max HP (at use), the turn it takes effect | record the user and the turn, by the user's max HP | Wish | agree |
| Substitute's HP | 1/4 of max HP at the start | same | presence (how much is left after hits is unknowable to the player) | agree |
| the move of Encore / Disable / a Choice lock | at the start (`onStart: move`) | the timers record the move at the time | yes | agree |
| Stockpile's layers and the Defense / Sp. Def actually raised | each layer records the stages actually raised at its start (`effectState.def / spd`); Spit Up / Swallow undo that many | layers (`-start stockpileN`); the engine undoes as many stages as there are layers | layers | layers agree; **engine gap**: a layer stacked at +6 raises nothing, Showdown does not undo it, the engine undoes it anyway (rare) |
| which stat Protosynthesis / Quark Drive boosts, whether from Booster Energy | the highest stat and the source at activation | `frombooster`; the boosted stat given by the stat name in `-activate` | yes | agree |
| the Metronome item's consecutive count | at each use | `consecutive`, `metronome_gone` | yes | agree |
| Slow Start's remaining turns | 5 on entry | `slowstart` | yes | agree |
| Supreme Overlord's fainted count | on entry (`onStart: fallen`) | the current fainted count | yes | in singles no teammate faints while it is active, so it equals the count on entry |
| Toxic's counter, consecutive Protect's counter, Future Sight's turn and slot | at the start / each time | timers | yes | agree |
| the length of confusion, sleep, locking moves (Outrage), partial trapping | random at the start | only presence and turns elapsed recorded | presence | hidden information; the engine expands it by probability |
| partial trapping's turns and damage (Grip Claw: 8 turns; Binding Band: 1/6) | the user's item at the start (`durationCallback`, `boundDivisor`) | the engine's fixed 5 turns, 1/8 | — | **engine gap** (not modelled) |
| the multiplier of Fury Cutter / Echoed Voice, the hit count of Rollout / Ice Ball | accumulated over consecutive uses | the engine only has the move data | — | **engine gap** (not modelled; these moves are rare in OU / random battles) |
| Laser Focus (a guaranteed critical hit next turn) | at the start | the engine has the volatile, no guaranteed critical hit | — | **engine gap** |
| Uproar's lock (3 turns, trapped during it, cannot switch) | at the start | the engine only has the volatile, no lock, no trapping: it offers switch options (found by C's round-trip check: these then fail to convert to commands) | — | **engine gap** |
| Magic Coat's Prankster, Roost's types, Counter / Mirror Coat's damage, Focus Punch / Shell Trap, Helping Hand, the boosts of Opportunist / Mirror Herb / White Herb, Eject Pack | this turn / on the spot | do not cross decision points | — | no rebuilding needed |
| Dragon Cheer, Ally Switch, Dynamax, G-Max, Bide | — | — | — | doubles or not Gen 9 |

Conclusion: the values that cross decision points and that we need to rebuild are all recorded when the event happens, and A's million-scale diff covers the observable part of them; what is left are the few the engine does not model (the partial-trapping items, consecutive-use multipliers, Laser Focus, the Stockpile layer at +6), engine gaps, to be handled separately under the rule "engine issues get dual tests first" (also: C's round-trip check found Uproar's lock, another engine gap).
